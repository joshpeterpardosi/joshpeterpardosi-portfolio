Retail Sales Forecasting — Time Series

Python · Pandas · statsmodels · PostgreSQL · Power BI

Built an end-to-end pipeline on Corporación Favorita retail sales — Pandas cleaning, a PostgreSQL data model, and a four-page Power BI dashboard covering store performance, promotion effects, and a 90-day forecast.

Holt-Winters additive forecasting reduced MAE by 37.6% against a 7-day moving-average baseline.

RMSE improved far less than MAE, indicating the model still under-forecasts sudden demand spikes — so the forecast is framed as a planning baseline, not an exact prediction.

From mathematics to data

My background is mathematics. Studying it, and then spending over two years teaching UTBK/SNBT mathematics to more than twenty students, trained two things I still rely on every day: working a problem in steps that can be checked, and explaining a quantitative result to someone who has no reason to trust it yet.
