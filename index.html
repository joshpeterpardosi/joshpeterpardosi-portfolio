04 — About

From mathematics to data.

A UTBK/SNBT mathematics session at SMA Unggul Del — taught through Bimbel Talenta Medan, 2026

My background is mathematics. Studying it, and then spending over two years teaching UTBK/SNBT mathematics to more than twenty students at Bimbel Talenta Medan — including sessions at SMA Unggul Del through the two institutions' partnership — trained two things I still rely on every day: working a problem in steps that can be checked, and explaining a quantitative result to someone who has no reason to trust it yet.

Data is where I spend my time now, and where I want my career to go. Most of that time goes into SQL and Python — pulling the data, cleaning it, testing whether a difference is real, then building the dashboard that makes the answer obvious.

Alongside it I've been learning to work with AI tools, which I treat as part of adapting to how this field actually operates today: I use them for code and for pressure-testing my own reasoning, and verify what they return before it counts. What I'm looking for is a Data Analyst or Data Scientist internship where all of this runs on data a team actually depends on.
