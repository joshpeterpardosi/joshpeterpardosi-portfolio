01 · MLOps · Python, Dagster, MLflow, LightGBM, FastAPI, Evidently, Docker

Churn prediction & revenue forecasting pipeline

Dagster orchestrates ingestion through training; MLflow promotes models by metric, FastAPI serves them, and an Evidently drift sensor triggers retraining.

Result 0.80 recall Threshold set by the cost of a missed churner, not left at 0.5. ROC-AUC 0.84; 107 tests in CI.
Limit 0.50 precision Half the flagged list are false alarms. Forecast RMSE 38.96 barely beats the naive 39.06.

03 — About

From mathematics to data.

My background is mathematics. Over two years teaching UTBK/SNBT mathematics to more than twenty students at Bimbel Talenta Medan — including sessions at SMA Unggul Del through the two institutions' partnership — trained two habits I still rely on: working a problem in steps that can be checked, and explaining the answer so someone else can follow it.

Most of my time now goes into SQL and Python — pulling the data, cleaning it, testing whether a difference is real, then building the dashboard that makes the answer obvious. I use AI tools for code and for pressure-testing my reasoning, and verify what they return before it counts.

What I'm looking for is a Data Analyst or Data Scientist internship where this work runs on data a team actually depends on.
